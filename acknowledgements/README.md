# Acknowledgements

Alexandre Dulaunoy conceived the Synthetic Exercise World Format and did the initial work to develop it. The idea grew out of a practical problem he encountered in cyber threat intelligence (CTI) exercises and work on open CTI standards. Both could benefit from neutral, reusable examples grounded in a fictional world.

Jean-Louis Huynen helped clarify the threat actor database. Participants at hackathon.lu in 2026 shared feedback and ideas. Thanks in particular to Philippe Parage and Benjamin Collas from the Range42.lu team.

The CIRCL team, including Aurélien Thirion and Sami Mokaddem, contributed ideas and reviewed the work. Many other members of the MISP project core team also shared ideas and reviewed the material.

Thank you to everyone who took part in earlier CTI exercises, gave feedback, and shared clever ideas. Their contributions helped shape the project.
